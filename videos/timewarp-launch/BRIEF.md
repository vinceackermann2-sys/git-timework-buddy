## Intent

The launch video for Timewarp, a desktop app whose AI agents use your computer
(browser, files, connected apps) to do the work for you. The user's own script:

1. Open on how much of life goes to work — "For 45 years, a third of your waking
   hours go to work." — over a montage of clicking, typing, working on a computer.
2. "Introducing Timewarp. Anything you can do on a computer, Timewarp does for you."
3. Example 1 — "Find business leads for me" → show it in the app doing exactly that.
4. Example 2 — another common business pain (user: "continue on the business work
   theme"). Chosen: "Three clients haven't paid. Chase them." → overdue invoices
   found, polite reminders drafted for approval. Swappable at the storyboard review.
5. Example 3 — the user asks for a coffee and Timewarp purchases it safely; touch on
   the safety vault.
6. End on "Work at the speed of light."

Feel: like the references the user supplied — light, airy, UI-led kinetic type,
floating product cards, a cursor, soft sounds, logo close. Every reference was
analysed frame by frame (scratchpad `refs/<name>/analysis.md`).

## Notes

- No invented statistics, testimonials or time-saving numbers. The opener's
  "a third of your waking hours for 45 years" is the agreed accurate line
  (~90,000 working hours over ages 20–65).
- Vault facts verified in code: device-only, encrypted locally, never uploaded;
  saved cards are stored without their security code. User confirmed agents ask before paying with a Vault card — show the approval card and "Agents ask before they pay."
- End card (user-revised): logo + wordmark, "Available for Mac & Windows", timewarpdev.com. No mascots and no pricing line on the end card.
- The music files are YouTube downloads; the user was told to confirm rights before
  posting publicly.
- Example companies/people/prices in demos are illustrative (site uses "Lumen Co.").
