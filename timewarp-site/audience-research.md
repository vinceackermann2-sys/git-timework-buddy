# Audience and positioning research

Research date: 5 October 2026.

## The first audience

Independent marketing consultants who operate a solo client-service business, already use AI chat, and assemble proposals, campaign reports, and research themselves.

This is a chosen initial segment within a broader potential market of independent professionals and owner-led service businesses. It is not a numerical TAM estimate. We do not have customer interviews, conversion data, or a validated estimate of how many buyers fit the segment.

Working persona: **Alex, the independent marketing consultant**. Alex is a composite for copy decisions, not a real customer or testimonial. Alex values autonomy, owns client strategy, works in browser tools and local documents, and has limited capacity for recurring assembly work. Proposed jobs and objections are hypotheses inferred from the research and product capabilities. No age, income, geography, client count, or time-saving figure is invented.

## Evidence and its limits

1. [Intuit QuickBooks Small Business Insights, July 2026 wave](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-insights/). The survey reports that 80% of US businesses with 0–100 employees regularly use AI and that marketing is a leading use case. It identifies privacy/security and understanding costs and benefits as adoption barriers. This supports an AI-familiar entry point and practical value messaging. It does not establish those percentages for solo marketing consultants or for Timewarp users.

2. [Leapers 2025 Mental Health in Freelancing Report, published January 2026](https://www.leapers.co/research/2025/report/). Respondents reported pressure around client communication and financial uncertainty; the report also describes administrative work during periods without billable work. This supports sensitivity to the burden of running an independent business. The sample is broader than marketing consultants and does not prove that software saves evenings or improves mental health. No mental-health outcome is claimed on the page.

3. [Anthropic Claude Cowork product page, reviewed October 2026](https://claude.com/product/cowork). Browser use, file work, connectors, reviewable deliverables, and agents are already marketed by a major competitor. These capabilities alone are therefore not a defensible market-exclusive claim. Timewarp is presented through a concrete workflow and its actual local-workspace and funding choices, without calling it the only agent product.

4. [Timewarp product README](../README.md). Source for local harness behavior, browser/file workflows, connected apps, local agent memory, cloud model processing, eligible connected Codex funding, and credit-funded dictation. The current Windows build is not described as production-ready, and a shipped Mac installer is not verified. The website leaves both installer links unset, as requested.

## Positioning

The promise is a lighter working day, with more attention available for client strategy. The mechanism is an agent working across a browser, local files, and connected apps to produce a saved draft in the same workspace. Visitors supply context and review the result.

**Market saturation / sophistication: high; stage 4 is a working marketing interpretation.** Buyers can encounter many AI productivity promises and established agent products. Avoid a generic “save 10 hours” claim. Lead with a narrow job, show the steps, and connect the outcome to the persona. This stage assignment is a positioning judgment, not a measured market statistic.

**Awareness: solution-aware, unfamiliar with Timewarp.** The page assumes Alex knows AI tools and their problem, but needs to understand why this workflow is useful. The demo explains execution before features. This awareness level is a testable targeting hypothesis, not an observed fact about every visitor.

## The requested criteria

The red thread is one list of five chores for one illustrative Lumen Co. proposal. In "The problem" the consultant does four of them and ChatGPT does one, while the clock runs to 21:48. In "How Timewarp works" an agent ticks off the same five, and the consultant reviews and approves. "The result" shows the day that frees up. Every section uses the same pattern: label, Quadrant Text headline and one visual. The brand matches the app and logo: purple `#8C14FF` and its logo tints, ink `#172033`, the app's composer, and the Orbit, Nova and Cosmo mascots.

## Validation to do after launch

Interview consultants who produce recurring proposals/reports, ask what they currently assemble by hand, and observe one real workflow. Validate whether the task can be completed reliably in the released app. Replace the illustrative demo with a consented, verified product recording when available. Measure successful downloads and first completed tasks before narrowing the persona further.
